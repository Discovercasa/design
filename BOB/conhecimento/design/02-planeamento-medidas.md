# 02 · Planning, circulation, ergonomics — and the reference measures
Rulebook v1.0 (2026-11-27). Manual v1.0: ch. 4 and Anexo A. Tags: see `../design-interiores.md`.

> **Every measure here is a general design and ergonomics reference** (manual «Nota técnica»): it never replaces law,
> regulations, accessibility standards, the specialists' design or the makers' instructions. In doubt, the technical
> validation for the site prevails [OBR B2]. Otherwise these are starting points [REC]/[CTX], not limits.

## 4.1 Planning method [REC]
Solve the layout before the decoration:
1. List the room's activities and their priority.
2. Set the functional zones and how they relate.
3. Place the fixed elements and the largest furniture first.
4. Check passages, openings, approach to equipment and use areas.
5. Adjust proportions and distances before choosing decorative pieces.
6. Confirm the layout in plan and, when possible, in 3D.

## 4.2 Circulation
- [REC] **90 cm** comfortable passage — aim for it on the main routes whenever the space allows.
- [CTX] **75 cm** compromise for a constrained route — can be tight; not a guarantee of accessibility or compliance.
- [TEC] Openings: check the area taken with the door/drawer open and a person using it.
- [CTX] Judge passages in context: a secondary route, an entrance, a busy passage and an appliance's use area do not need
  the same.
- (DC) Our interior doors are usually 2,00 × 0,80 m (`sistema-construtivo.md`, user 2026-10-09) and interior walls take
  doors only — use them as the scale check in every room.

## 4.3 Living room
- [EST] Do not force the sofa to at most two thirds of the wall: it may take 80 % or more if scale, circulation and
  balance allow.
- [REC] Sofa to coffee table from 40–45 cm, adjusted to the sofa's depth, reach and the passage needed.
- [EST] A coffee table about two thirds of the sofa's length is a first reference, not a duty; the shapes change it.
- [CTX] TV viewing distance is not a fixed multiple of the diagonal: consider diagonal, resolution, viewing angle,
  mounting height, content and preference.
- [REC] The sofa never blocks doors, openings, radiators, access to terraces or main routes.
- [REC] Relate sofa, armchairs, side table, reading light and the room's focal point.

## 4.4 Dining
- [REC] About 90 cm from the table edge to the wall or obstacle, to pull a chair out and pass.
- [REC] About 60 cm of table width per person, adjusted to the table's shape and the chairs.
- [REC] Check the passage behind a seated person, above all when the table sits between kitchen and living room.
- [CTX] Round, oval, square or rectangular by the room's geometry, the capacity wanted and how it is used.

## 4.5 Rugs
- [REC] Living room: the front feet of the sofa and armchairs on the rug, when the composition allows.
- [EST] The rug ties the seating group together — never a small island alone in the middle.
- [REC] Under a bed: about 50–60 cm of rug showing on each side and, when possible, at the foot.
- [CTX] Consider doors, wardrobes, tripping, cleaning and the floor type before size and thickness.

## 4.6 Bedrooms
- [REC] 60–70 cm free on each side of the bed as a first reference; 75–90 cm when the area allows.
- [REC] About 90 cm in front of the wardrobe, checking door swing and where the user stands.
- [TEC] Hinged wardrobe doors need swing and approach space; sliding doors need less to open but still need room to use
  the inside.
- [REC] Check access to bedside tables, sockets, switches, reading light and the route to the door.
- [REC] Size the bed and furniture to the usable area, not the room's gross size.

## 4.7 Kitchens
- [CTX] Worktop height near 90 cm and depth near 60 cm are common references, not universal: adjust to stature,
  ergonomics, equipment and the spec.
- [REC] About 100–120 cm between worktop and island; more with two people cooking, appliance doors open or a busy route.
- [CTX] 60 cm modules are very common but not mandatory: 30, 40, 45, 60, 80, 90 cm and others exist by system and maker.
- [CTX] Use the fridge–sink–hob triangle as a guide where the layout favours it, not as a rule in linear, island or
  complex kitchens.
- [REC] Organise by zones: food storage, washing, preparation, cooking, storage/support.
- [TEC] Fridge, oven, dishwasher, drawers and doors open without collisions.
- [REC] Landing space next to hob, sink and fridge; never sacrifice prep surface just to get a bigger island.
- [TEC] Extraction, ventilation, task light, sockets and technical needs: validated by a specialist.

## 4.8 Bathrooms
- [REC] WC axis from about 40–45 cm off the side wall; confirm the model, the installation and the real clearance.
- [REC] About 60–75 cm free in front of the WC as a first guide — not an accessibility requirement.
- [CTX] Washbasin about 80–85 cm high is common; adjust to user, basin type, unit and tap.
- [REC] A shower of about 90 × 120 cm tends to be comfortable; 80 × 80 cm is very compact, not an ideal.
- [TEC] Check the swing of screens, doors, drawers and access to taps and niches.
- [TEC] Materials fit for humidity, cleaning and slip resistance; confirm performance and installation with the product.
- (DC) [TEC] Wall-hung pieces (suspended WC with frame, wall-hung vanity, tall wall units, TV): check how they fix in our
  timber-frame walls (method §9, `metodo/07-paredes-exterior-interior.md`) before drawing them; the method holds no rule
  for these fixings yet — ask the user if a design depends on it.

## 4.9 Curtains, art and pendants
| Element | First reference | Adjust to |
|---|---|---|
| Pendant over table/island | 75–90 cm from top to the lamp's underside | lamp size, ceiling height, sight lines, users' height, light spread |
| Single picture | centre at about 145–150 cm from the floor | over a sofa or unit: relate it to the furniture and composition |
| Curtain rod | near the ceiling or about 15 cm above the opening | the architecture, ceiling height, pelmets |
| Curtain width | about 15–25 cm past each side of the opening | more to stack the fabric clear of the glass, or for the composition |
| Curtain length | about 1 cm above the floor | may touch or pool if a conscious, functional choice |
All [REC]/[EST].
