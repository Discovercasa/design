The user works at DiscoverCasa (Penafiel, Porto district, Portugal; discovercasa.pt). The company designs, licenses and builds houses from scratch and hands them over ready to live in ("chave na mão"). Its main differentiator in Portugal is building with a **timber structure**, which sits on a **concrete foundation base**. It has 15+ years of experience, builds north to south including the islands, and offers a 10-year guarantee.

Standard system as described on the company's model pages (checked 2026-09-23):
- External walls: posts and beams ("pilares e vigas") in solid Nordic pine plus glulam ("pinho nórdico maciço e lamelados").
- Exterior finish: timber cladding ("forro de madeira"), double-ventilated, over a waterproof membrane. Options: thermo-treated cork, ETICS ("capoto") or stone.
- Interior: plasterboard ("pladur") on walls and ceilings, floating floors, ceramic tiles in kitchens and bathrooms. Options: OSB, Viroc, cork, timber lining.
- Insulation in every cavity (external walls, internal walls, roof). They claim energy ratings up to A++.
- Windows: aluminium with thermal break and double glazing, or PVC.
- DiscoverCasa handles the architecture and engineering projects and the licensing.

The actual on-site build-up the user described on 2026-09-24 is in the repo, in `conhecimento/sistema-construtivo.md`, with the model defaults in `ferramentas/modelo/sistema_construtivo.json`. It covers:
- outside of the walls: OSB 9, then Traspir 95, then a finish chosen by the client (Tremolo Douglas wood, ETICS or cork);
- inside: Volcalis wool, OSB 15 and Pladur;
- floors: vinyl;
- roof: sandwich panel.
