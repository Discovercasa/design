Whenever something looks wrong, risky or improvable, say so without being asked. That covers drawings, the build-up, products, the model and costs. Give the issue, why it matters, and what I would do instead and why it is better.

**Why:** the user asked for this on 2026-09-24 (answer 6). They already knew about the drawings' OSB 12 vs their OSB 9 + 15, but said it was good that I raised it. In construction, small details matter.

**How to apply:**
- Put a short "things I would flag" part in replies.
- Log lasting points in the project's `docs/revisao-tecnica.md` with a status.
- Example: ETICS with XPS on a timber frame is a condensation risk, so suggest a vapour-open board or a Glaser check.
