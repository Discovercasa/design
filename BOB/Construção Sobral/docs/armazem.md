# Warehouse ("Edifício armazém / apoio agrícola") — facts and redesign notes

Drawing: `desenhos/PD_Estabilidade Armazém.dwfx`, one A1 sheet at 1:100, "PROJETO: Construção de 16
moradias unifamiliares e 1 edifício de armazém". Reinforced-concrete design; DiscoverCasa intends to
**rebuild it in timber** as shared facilities for residents (user mentioned: group room, gym,
refrigerated store — mapping to wings not confirmed yet).

## As drawn (concrete)
Three wings (each ≈ 6 × 10 m, ≈ 60 m²) around a "Pátio exterior" (46.9 m², granite paving / troweled concrete):
- North wing, pillars P25–P32 (grid ≈ 3.4 / 3.4 / 2.85 m): Ferramentaria – material e equipamento
  agrícola 22 m²; I.S. vestiário 6.4 m² and 7 m²; antecâmara vestiário (cacifos) 14.8 m².
- West wing, P11–P21 (grid ≈ 2.8 / 3.9 / 2.8 m): Armazém (frio) 8.2 m²; Triagem e preparação de fruta
  38 m²; Armazém 8.2 m².
- East wing, P1–P10, P22–P24 (grid ≈ 3.1 / 3.2 / 3.5 m): Sala de condomínio / refeitório / sala de
  convívio 26.8 m²; Cozinha 15 m²; Escada 9.3 m² — **over a lower floor** (201.23): Garagem (trator,
  carrinha) 35.1 m², entrada/escada 9.3 m², Q.E., caixas de correio (17 units), covered entrance.
- Levels: upper floor 204.50, lower floor 201.23 (3.27 m, 18 risers 0.18 × 0.28). Terrain contours
  ≈ 200.35–202.81; lot limit drawn.
- Structure: pillars 25×40 (arm. long. 6Ø12…), foundation tie beams CB.1.1 40×40 (2Ø12 top, 3Ø12 bottom,
  Ø8//25), pad footings 90×90 to 120×120 × 40 (3–4Ø16//30), slabs h 20–22 cm Ø10//15 (roof slabs 20 cm),
  small slabs h 16 Ø6//15, punching reinforcement at pillars, raft 20 cm where floors bear on the ground.
- Site works on the same sheet: RC retaining wall on the lot boundary (3.00 m high, base 2.10 × 0.60,
  Ø12//0.2, timber-post fence with mesh on top); fence wall with 25×25 pillars every 3.0 m, block
  infill, coping/mid beams 20×16 (4Ø8, Ø6//15). Materials C25/30 XC2, A400NR.

## Notes for the timber redesign
- Anything in contact with soil stays concrete: footings/raft, the east wing's lower storey walls
  that retain earth (3.27 m level difference), the boundary retaining wall.
- New uses change imposed loads (EN 1991-1-1): gym/assembly areas are category C (≥ 4–5 kN/m²),
  far above housing; check floor over the garage accordingly.
- Refrigerated store: vapour flows from outside to inside → vapour barrier on the OUTER (warm) side,
  or a prefabricated insulated cold room inside a timber shell; protect timber from condensation.
- Flat concrete roofs (20 cm slabs) → timber roof needs a real fall (≥ 2 %) and careful waterproofing,
  or pitched roofs matching the houses.
